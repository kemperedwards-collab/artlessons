About Your Instructor

Art taught in a
personal, unhurried space.

Welcome! I offer private art lessons out of my home studio in Sewickley, bringing a warm, studio-school atmosphere right to your neighborhood. My approach is rooted in careful observation — learning to truly see before you draw.

Whether you're picking up a pencil for the first time or returning to a practice you left behind, lessons are tailored to your pace, your interests, and the media that excite you most.

Students range from young beginners to working adults. Every session is one-on-one: no waiting, no distractions, just you and the work in front of you.
